Current lesson — grammar

A. Noun + zěnmeyàng? = "How is/was ___?" — Nǐ de kè zěnmeyàng? · Nǐ de kǎoshì zěnmeyàng? · Nǐ de gōngzuò zěnmeyàng?
B. gānggāng + verb = just did something (a moment ago). Wǒ gānggāng dào jiā. · With zài for an ongoing action just now: Nǐ gānggāng zài zuò shénme? = What were you doing just now? → Wǒ gānggāng zài zuò kāfēi. / Wǒ gānggāng zài shuìjiào.
Contrast to drill: gānggāng (just now, minutes ago) vs zuótiān (yesterday) vs yǐjīng (already).
C. dào + place (+ le) = arrive at. Wǒ zuótiān dào jiā le. · Xià ge xīngqī wǒ dào Bìlǔ. (= I arrive in Peru next week.)
D. wèishénme = why. Sits where the answer goes, no inversion: Wèishénme nǐ de kè dōu shì shuǐkè? Answered with yīnwèi.
E. Subject + dōu shì + noun = all of them are ___. Tāmen shì Zhōngguó rén → Tāmen dōu shì Zhōngguó rén (they are ALL Chinese). · Zhèxiē diāosù dōu shì wǒ de. = These sculptures are all mine. · Zhèxiē dōu shì wǔ yuán, nàxiē dōu shì shí yuán.
dōu goes BEFORE the verb, never after the subject-noun it quantifies.
F. yīnwèi = because. Jīntiān wǒ zài jiā, yīnwèi jīntiān shì xīngqīliù.
Pairs with the known weak spot: yīnwèi…suǒyǐ (causal) vs suīrán…dànshì (concessive) — drill the contrast.
G. bù + adjective = not ___. bù zhòngyào, bù wúliáo (Jīntiān bù wúliáo.)
